Expressed milk now has a screen of its own. Log a pumping session: how much, when, the side, and where the milk went — fridge, freezer, room temperature or straight to the baby. Above the history, the milk stash shows what is actually available in each place, leaving out milk already given and anything kept past the usual times, and a batch is marked used in one tap. On the widget, a bottle no longer hides which breast comes next: it also shows the side the last breastfeed started on.
