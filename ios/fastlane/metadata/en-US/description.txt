Sprout is a calm, private log for a newborn's first weeks and months, and for the parent recovering from the birth.

Everything stays on your iPhone. There's no account to create, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. Sprout makes no network connections at all: nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it.

WHAT YOU CAN LOG

• Feeds: breast, bottle and solids. A live breastfeeding timer switches sides with one tap, and can pause for a burp without counting the break as feeding.
• Pumping: how much, which side and where the milk went, with a milk stash that adds up what's in the fridge and the freezer.
• Sleep: naps and nights, and where your baby slept if you want to note it.
• Diapers: wet, dirty or both, with a stool color scale.
• Growth: weight, height and head circumference, read against the WHO growth standards.
• Medicines: scheduled treatments with reminders, and medicines given as needed, with the wait between doses you were told to keep.
• Your recovery: a short daily check-in on mood, and on healing and breast comfort when they apply to you. You can turn it off at any time.

FOR REAL FAMILIES

• Several babies, twins included, side by side.
• Share a baby's record with the other phones in your household by sending a file. No account and no server; the shared record is encrypted.
• Daily and weekly statistics, and a PDF report or spreadsheet to bring to an appointment, which you can lock with a password.
• Optional reminders for feeds and treatments, scheduled on the phone itself.
• In English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Portuguese.

Sprout keeps a record of what you enter. It gives no medical advice and does not replace a health professional.

Sprout is free, open-source software (GNU GPLv3), with no ads and no in-app purchases.
