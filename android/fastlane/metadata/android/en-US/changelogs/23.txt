Two breastfeeds that were really one can now be joined. If you saved after the first side and started the second a few minutes later, a small Join with previous appears on the second feed. It asks first, then keeps the minutes between them as a break, as if you had pressed Pause: the feed count and the time since the last feed count one feed again, and the time at the breast doesn't change. Only offered for feeds less than half an hour apart, and never suggested.
