Logging something after the fact? You can now pick the date, not just the time — feeds, sleep, diapers and wellbeing entries can be filed on the day they actually happened. Deleting an entry now asks first, so a mis-tap while holding the baby doesn't cost you the log. And the home-screen widget covers every kind of feed, names the baby it belongs to, and updates live instead of getting stuck on what it first read.
