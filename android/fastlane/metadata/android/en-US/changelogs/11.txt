You can now share a baby's record with the people looking after them — the other parent, a grandparent — with no account and no cloud. One of you sends an invitation file through whatever you already use; after that the phones exchange encrypted entries that merge without duplicating or overwriting anything. On Android 12+ it can also happen on its own over Bluetooth when you are in the same room with the app open. Your own check-ins never leave your phone.
