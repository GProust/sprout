A new Statistics screen, one tap from the dashboard, shows what the last 7, 30 or 90 days added up to: feeds and how much of each kind, hours asleep and how often your baby settled, wet and dirty changes — a bar per day, and a daily average that leaves out the day still being lived. Weight, length and head circumference are drawn over the WHO growth curves; Sprout still never asks your baby's sex, so it reads both references. Tap a day for its detail. Solids can now be weighed in grams.
